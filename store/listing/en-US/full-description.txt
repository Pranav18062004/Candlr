Keep a little book of your people.

Candlr is a quiet, offline place to remember birthdays. Its warm Paper and Evening themes feel at home beside a favorite book, with subtle motion and a simple, uncluttered layout.

YOUR PEOPLE, CLOSE AT HAND
Save names, birthdays, optional birth years, photos, and personal notes. Bookmark people, search your book, and see what is coming up or browse by month.

A GENTLE REMINDER
Choose local birthday reminders on the day or 1, 3, or 7 days before. Set a reminder time and individual preferences for each person. Choose how to celebrate February 29 birthdays in other years.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No purchases. No analytics. Candlr has no internet permission, and your book stays in the app's private storage on your device.

BACK UP ON YOUR TERMS
Create a backup wherever Android's file picker lets you save it. Restore by merging with your current book or replacing it, with a local recovery copy of the previous book. Backups include names, notes, photos, and preferences and are not password protected. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider handles any transfer; Candlr itself does not connect to the cloud.

COMFORTABLE TO USE
Choose Paper, Evening, or your system theme. Reduce motion in the app or use Android's animation settings. Candlr supports Android 8.0 and newer.

Reminders are approximate: Android battery saving and device restrictions may delay delivery. After force-stopping the app, open it again to resume reminders. Save a backup before changing phones or uninstalling.
