Candlr is a small, completely offline birthday book for Android 8 and later.

- Warm Paper and Evening themes with subtle, accessibility-aware motion.
- Birthdays, notes, photos, bookmarks, calendar, and local reminders.
- Manual backup and restore to a location you choose.
- No accounts, ads, purchases, analytics, or app network permission.

Release preparation includes retained tab state, guarded editor drafts, accessible calendar targets, cached photos with orphan cleanup, and improved notification wording.

**Draft — not yet ready for public installation.** The owner is configuring the permanent signing key. A verified release-signed APK and its checksums will be attached after signing and final physical-device checks. No debug-signed APK is offered as a production download.

Reminder timing remains subject to Android and manufacturer power-management restrictions. Keep a manual backup before changing phones or uninstalling.
