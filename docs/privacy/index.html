Candlr privacy policy

Candlr is developed by Pranav Nambiar. Contact: pranavnambiar12345@gmail.com.

Your birthday book stays on your device. Candlr stores the names, birthday dates, optional birth years, notes, bookmarks, reminder preferences, and photos you choose to enter. It uses them only to display your book and schedule local birthday reminders.

Candlr has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or network permission. The app does not collect or transmit your book to the developer or to any server. Photos are copied into private app storage, resized, and saved without the original EXIF metadata. Originals in your photo library are not changed.

Notifications are optional. Android may display a person's name on a notification; hide notification content in Android settings if desired. Photo and backup access is limited to the files you choose through Android's picker. No contacts or broad photo-library permission is requested.

Backups are created only when you request them. A backup includes your birthdays, notes, photos, and preferences, and is not password protected. You choose where to save it. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider handles any upload under its own policy; Candlr itself has no cloud connection. Android automatic app backup and device transfer are disabled.

Deleting a birthday removes it from the active book. An immediate undo temporarily preserves its photo. Replacing or removing photos reclaims unused copies. One unfinished draft photo may be retained for Android to restore the editor after process death. Restore keeps one local recovery archive containing the previous book until the next restore replaces it. Exported backups and the recovery archive may still contain entries deleted from the active book.

To erase all local app data, including drafts and the recovery archive, use Android Settings > Apps > Candlr > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall Candlr without selecting the option to keep app data. Some Android versions offer that option during uninstall. Delete exported backups separately from the destinations you chose. The developer cannot access or recover your book. Keep a backup before changing phones or uninstalling.

If you email for support, your email provider transmits that message and the developer receives the information you choose to send. Avoid including birthday books or other people's personal information unless necessary. The app does not send support messages automatically.

This policy describes version 1.0.0. Any future change to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy. Last updated: 17 September 2026.
